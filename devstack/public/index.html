Local dev stack. ma-stream resolves a magnet into an id plus the peer and chunk records; sl-stream streams the video from those records over the BitTorrent peer wire.

1 · Resolve a magnet

A resolve walks trackers, the DHT and PEX while fetching metadata over BEP-9, then verifies SHA-1(info dict) === infohash before writing anything.

The torrent's contents appear here.

Every file the torrent contains, in the order sl-stream's ?file= indexes them. Pick any playable one — the pieces are shared, so switching costs no rediscovery.

3 · Play

Nothing loaded. Everything plays through libmedia — Matroska, AVI, HEVC, AC-3, DTS and the rest are decoded in WebAssembly, and the source is still fetched by range.
